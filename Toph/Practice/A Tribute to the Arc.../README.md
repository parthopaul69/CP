Long before terms like “IT” and “Digital Bangladesh” became part of everyday conversation, National Professor Dr. Jamilur Reza Choudhury was already helping build the foundations of Bangladesh’s technological future.

As the Director of the BUET Computer Centre from 1982 to 1992, he played a pioneering role in introducing computing to a generation of Bangladeshi engineers at a time when computers were still unfamiliar to most people in the country. His vision and commitment to education continued throughout his distinguished career. He served as the founding Vice-Chancellor of BRAC University and later as the Vice-Chancellor of the University of Asia Pacific, where he continued to inspire and mentor students, teachers, and researchers until his final days.

JRC Sir believed that technology, guided by discipline, knowledge, and vision, could become a powerful force for transforming a nation. The opportunities available to Bangladeshi students in computing today are, in part, built upon the foundations laid by pioneers like him, who recognized the importance of connecting engineering education with emerging technologies.

In his memory, we the students, colleagues, and successors who carry forward his legacy, dedicate this small programming challenge to him. As you solve the problems ahead, we invite you to remember the vision, curiosity, and dedication that defined his remarkable journey.

There is no special algorithm required to honor his legacy only your attention, your effort, and your appreciation for the path he helped build.

Input

This problem has no input.

Output

For this Problem, Just print "**Long Live the Legacy of JRC Sir!**" (without quotes).
